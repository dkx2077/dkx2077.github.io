    Appendix: the pelicans

    In October 2024 Simon Willison began asking every new language model to Generate an SVG of a pelican riding a bicycle. It was meant half as a joke, and it became the first thing many people try on a new model. His running collection is the reference.

    Models answer that prompt once. This page answers it about twenty times and gets most of them wrong on purpose. Each entry below is graded on the three things the prompt asks for.
